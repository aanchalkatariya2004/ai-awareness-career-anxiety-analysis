# Impact of AI Awareness on Career Anxiety Among Students

## Overview
This project explores the relationship between AI awareness and career anxiety among students using statistical analysis techniques. The study examines how students from different educational backgrounds and age groups perceive Artificial Intelligence and its impact on their future careers.

## Objectives
- Measure the level of AI awareness among students
- Analyze career anxiety related to AI growth
- Study the relationship between AI awareness and career anxiety
- Compare awareness and anxiety across demographic groups

## Dataset
- Sample Size: 102 Students
- Data collected through a structured questionnaire
- Variables included:
  - AI Awareness
  - Career Anxiety
  - Age Group
  - Education Level
  - Academic Stream

## Tools Used
- SPSS
- Microsoft Excel

## Statistical Techniques
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Descriptive Statistics
- Cronbach’s Alpha
- ANOVA
- Chi-Square Test
- Pearson Correlation

## Key Findings
- AI awareness and career anxiety showed a weak and statistically insignificant correlation.
- Significant differences in AI awareness were observed across education levels and age groups.
- Students reported moderate pressure to learn AI-related skills for employability.
- Confidence in adapting to AI changes significantly affected perceived skill pressure.

## Conclusion
The study suggests that AI awareness alone does not directly influence career anxiety. Factors such as adaptability confidence, education level, and exposure to technology play a more important role in shaping students’ perceptions toward AI-driven career changes.
